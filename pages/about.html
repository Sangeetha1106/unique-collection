Our Heritage & Craft

About UNIQUE COLLECTION

Celebrating feminine grace with authentic Indian ethnic attire and revolutionary 18K anti-tarnish waterproof jewellery.

Our Story

At UNIQUE COLLECTION, we believe every woman deserves to feel royal every day. Our journey started with a vision to make intricate Indian ethnic fashion and fine bridal craftsmanship accessible, paired with daily-wear jewellery that never tarnishes or loses its radiance.

From handpicked 2-piece and 3-piece churidar sets, bridal lehengas, and breathable 4-way stretch cotton leggings, to 18K PVD waterproof titanium steel kadas, rings, and micro gold-plated temple ornaments, our curated collections reflect impeccable standards and authentic Indian elegance.

✓

Authentic Artistry

Hand-tailored ethnic silhouettes, rich weaves, and timeless zardozi embroidery.

💧

100% Waterproof

18K PVD physical vapor deposition coating built for lifetime shine in water and perfume.

✨

1-Year Plating Guarantee

Reliable micro gold-plated covering ornaments with traditional 24K South Indian luster.
